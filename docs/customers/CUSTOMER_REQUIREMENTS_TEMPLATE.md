# TradesStack Customer Requirements Template

Use one row per material requirement. Link approved rows to the customer
implementation record and differences register.

## Classifications

- **CONFIGURATION** — supported by existing settings, data, permissions,
  templates or workflows.
- **CORE MAIN CAPABILITY** — broadly useful and should be built in TradesStack
  Main.
- **CONTROLLED EXTENSION** — genuinely customer-specific but isolated behind an
  approved boundary.
- **CUSTOMER-SPECIFIC CODE** — unavoidable unique source behavior; last resort.
- **OUT OF SCOPE** — not appropriate or not commercially justified.
- **FUTURE** — useful, but not required for the current implementation.

## Scope rules

### LAUNCH REQUIRED

Must be complete and accepted before go-live. Security, tenancy, migration,
release provenance and critical workflows are always launch-gating.

### IMPORTANT BUT NOT BLOCKING

Can follow shortly after launch with a named owner and target date.

### NICE TO HAVE

Should not delay implementation. Keep it visible without allowing scope creep.

### FUTURE

Post-launch roadmap work. Reassess after the customer has used the system.

## Commercial rules

No significant custom work begins until:

- the requirement is written;
- classification is agreed;
- scope and impact are understood;
- commercial treatment is agreed; and
- acceptance criteria exist.

If a customer request is useful to other customers, record it as a Main
capability candidate even when the first implementation is paid by one
customer.
