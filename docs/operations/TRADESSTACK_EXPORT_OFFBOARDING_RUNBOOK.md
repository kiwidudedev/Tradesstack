# TradesStack export and offboarding runbook

This runbook does not invent legal retention periods. Contract, legal,
accounting, QA and privacy decisions must be recorded by the owner.

1. Confirm customer, contract, scope, effective date and retention obligations.
2. Freeze releases and operational changes; record final client release,
   package set, config contract and migration target.
3. Export agreed structured business data, metadata, provenance and documents/
   Storage objects in the agreed encrypted format.
4. Verify counts, checksums, document versions, drawing evidence, QA evidence
   and accounting records; obtain customer confirmation.
5. Disconnect approved provider links, revoke users/access, rotate or retire
   client secrets and record each action.
6. Retain or delete data, logs, backups and immutable evidence according to the
   approved legal/business policy; do not silently delete historical records.
7. Retire, transfer or preserve exact resources; never wildcard-delete.
8. Record final export, deletion/transfer evidence, resource disposition and
   owner approval in the operating index.
